Storyline & Atmosphere: The Hybrid Journey

Defining the emotional arc and visual evolution of the experience

A

The "Transformation" Path

Starts in a void, building into complex organic life (research) and ending in a calm, settled state (archive/contact). Focuses on the evolution of ideas.

B

The "Deep Dive" Path

Starts on the surface (persona), diving deep into an "ocean" of data and particles (research), and resurfacing at the end. Focuses on discovery and exploration.

C

The "Starlight" Path

A journey through a cosmic nebula. Particles represent connections and publications. Focuses on infinite potential and network.
