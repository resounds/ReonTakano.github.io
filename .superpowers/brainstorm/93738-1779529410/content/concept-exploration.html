Design Concept: Immersion & Storytelling

Exploring the "teamLab" and "Disney" inspirations

A

teamLab: Generative Immersion

Interaction-driven, organic particles, reactive backgrounds that change based on user movement (mouse/scroll).

[Organic Particles & Reactive Flows]

B

Disney: Narrative Journey

Staging, character-led transitions, clear emotional arcs, and "scenes" that feel like chapters in a book.

[Staged Scenes & Animated Transitions]

C

Hybrid: Immersive Storyteller

A narrative path (Disney) where the environment reacts and evolves organically (teamLab) as you move through it.
